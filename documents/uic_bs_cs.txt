TITLE: BS in Computer Science - University of Illinois Chicago

The Bachelor of Science in Computer Science degree from UIC requires completion of university, college, and department requirements totaling 128 credit hours.

DEGREE REQUIREMENTS:
- Nonengineering and General Education: 46 hours
- Required College of Engineering Courses: 46 hours
- Technical Electives: 18 hours
- Required Mathematics Courses: 9 hours
- Free Electives: 9 hours
- Total: 128 hours

GENERAL EDUCATION & WRITING:
Students complete Academic Writing I: Writing in Academic and Public Contexts and Academic Writing II: Writing for Inquiry and Research, plus courses exploring world cultures, creative arts, history, individual/society, and U.S. society.

MATHEMATICS FOUNDATION:
The program requires calculus sequence (MATH 180, 181, 210) plus additional mathematics electives with at least one course taken from IE 342 or STAT 381.

ENGINEERING CORE COURSES:
Students take foundational programming (selecting from CS 111, 112, or 113), followed by core CS courses including data structures, machine organization, systems programming, computer design, algorithms, and software design.

TECHNICAL ELECTIVES:
Students select six courses from advanced offerings spanning artificial intelligence, machine learning, database systems, networking, security, compiler design, and specialized topics like visualization, game development, and cryptography.

SCIENCE REQUIREMENTS:
Two science courses must be completed from biology, chemistry, physics, or environmental science options.
